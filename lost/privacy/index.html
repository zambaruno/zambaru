Privacy Policy for LOST

Effective date: October 31, 2026

LOST is a lost-and-found service provided by Zambaruno LLC (“we,” “us,” or “our”). This policy explains how information may be collected, used, shared, and protected when you use the LOST website and mobile application, including test versions.

1. Information we collect

  • Account information: your email address, authentication identifiers, and any profile information you choose to provide when creating or accessing an account.
  • Posts and photographs: images, descriptions, and other information you submit about lost or found belongings.
  • Location: location details you choose to provide, and device location only if a feature requests it and you grant permission.
  • Technical information: device, app version, basic diagnostics, security and usage data needed to operate and improve the service.
  • Communications: messages or reports you send to us, including beta-testing feedback.

2. How we use information

We use information to provide account access, display lost-and-found listings, help users reconnect belongings with their owners, prevent misuse, maintain security, troubleshoot problems, and improve LOST.

3. Information visible to other users

Photos, descriptions, and location details included in public listings may be visible to other users or website visitors. Do not include passwords, government identification numbers, private addresses, or sensitive personal information in public posts. You should avoid photographing people or documents without appropriate permission.

4. Service providers and sharing

We may use third-party providers for hosting, authentication, database storage, app distribution, analytics, or advertising where those features are enabled. Providers process data as needed to deliver their services. We may disclose information where legally required, to protect rights and safety, or as part of a legitimate business transfer. We do not sell personal information as a standalone product.

5. Permissions

LOST may request access to your camera or photos to create listings, location to associate an item with an area, and other device capabilities for features you choose to use. You can manage permissions in your device settings. Some functions may not work without their relevant permissions.

6. Data retention and deletion

We retain information for as long as needed to provide the service, meet legal obligations, resolve disputes, and protect against abuse. You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information or account by contacting us at the address below. Some records may be retained where required by law or for legitimate security purposes.

7. Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational steps to protect information. No online service or transmission method can guarantee absolute security.

8. Children

LOST is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided such information, contact us so we can review and delete it as appropriate.

9. International users

Information may be processed in countries other than your own, subject to applicable law and the safeguards of our service providers.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as LOST evolves. The current version and its effective date will be posted on this page.
