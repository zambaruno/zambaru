Inventions / Product development

One-Hand Liquid Dispenser

Press and receive liquid with the same hand.

An independent mechanical dispenser concept designed to make everyday liquid dispensing easier. Its outlet is positioned near the pressing surface, allowing one hand to operate the pump and receive the dose.

Prototype stage · Manufacturing development planned

Concept illustration. Original prototype photograph to be added.

01 / Operation

One hand. Three simple steps.

01 · Position

Place your hand at the pump and outlet.

02 · Press

Activate the dispenser with that hand.

03 · Receive

Collect the dispensed liquid without using your other hand.
