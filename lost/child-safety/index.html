LOST — Child Safety Standards

Publisher: Zambaruno LLC
Application: LOST
Last updated: November 3, 2026

LOST is intended to help people report, find and return lost items. We prohibit any use of LOST to sexually exploit or abuse children.

Zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation

LOST prohibits child sexual abuse material (CSAM), grooming, sexual solicitation of minors, trafficking, and any content or conduct that facilitates the sexual exploitation or abuse of children. Users must not upload, share, request, promote or link to such material or activity.

Reporting concerns

To report suspected child sexual abuse or exploitation involving LOST, contact zambaruno@gmail.com with the subject “LOST Child Safety Report”. Include relevant account or post details and links where available. Do not email or redistribute illegal imagery. If a child is in immediate danger, contact local emergency services or law enforcement.

Response and enforcement

Reports will be reviewed, and content or accounts found to violate these standards may be removed or restricted. Where legally required, we will report suspected child sexual abuse material or exploitation to appropriate authorities and cooperate with lawful investigations.
