Delete your LOST account and data

LOST is a lost-and-found service operated by Zambaruno LLC. You can request deletion of your LOST account and associated personal data without installing or opening the app.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email from the address associated with your LOST account to zambaruno@gmail.com.
  2. Use the subject line LOST account deletion request.
  3. In the message, state that you want your LOST account and associated data deleted. You do not need to send a password or identification document.
  4. We will review the request and may ask you to verify ownership of the account before proceeding. We aim to complete verified deletion requests within 14 days of receipt, unless a lawful reason requires retaining specific records. We will confirm when the request has been handled.

Request account deletion by email

14-day deletion period

After we receive your request, there is a 14-day period to process deletion. You can cancel your request during that period by emailing zambaruno@gmail.com from your account email. Deletion is processed manually; the account is not automatically deleted when the email is sent. If we need to verify account ownership or must retain specific records for lawful reasons, we will explain the next steps.

What data is covered?

An approved account deletion request covers your LOST account and associated account identifiers, profile information, and content you submitted, including lost-and-found listings and uploaded photographs, subject to the exceptions below.

What may be retained?

Some information, such as records relevant to a reported fraudulent claim or disputed transfer of a lost item, may be retained where required by law or reasonably necessary for security, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, or other legitimate legal obligations. Any such retention is limited to what is necessary and is not indefinite.

This page describes the email-based deletion request process. It does not automatically delete an account when the email link is clicked.
